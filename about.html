Bringing devotion and convenience together for every home.

PurohitMitra is India's trusted platform that connects you with experienced and verified Purohits for all your pujas and rituals. We blend tradition with technology to make your spiritual journey simple, transparent and meaningful.

Our Story

In today's fast-paced world, finding the right Purohit for a puja can be a challenge. We started PurohitMitra with a simple thought – to make authentic rituals accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our platform was created to bridge the gap between tradition and modern convenience, ensuring every puja is performed with devotion, correct rituals and complete peace of mind.
