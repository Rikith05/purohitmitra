About Griha Pravesh Puja

Griha Pravesh Puja is an important Vedic ritual performed before entering a new home. It invokes divine blessings, removes Vastu doshas and fills the home with positivity, harmony and prosperity.

Benefits
  • Brings happiness, peace and prosperity
  • Removes negative energies and Vastu doshas
  • Enhances health, wealth and well-being
  • Invokes blessings of deities and ancestors
  • Creates positive environment for family growth
