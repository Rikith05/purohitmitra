Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a Purohit for a puja on PurohitMitra? +
Booking is simple and takes less than 2 minutes: 1) Browse our 40+ Vedic pujas or select a verified Purohit. 2) Pick your preferred date and time slot using our interactive calendar. 3) Choose your preferred Purohit based on reviews, languages, and dakshina. 4) Review your booking and complete payment via UPI or Cards to get instant confirmation.
Can I customize the Puja Date and Vedic Muhurtham timing? +
Yes! When booking any puja, click "Other (Custom Time)" in Step 1. You can choose from traditional Vedic Muhurtham presets (such as Brahma Muhurtham, Pratah Kala, Abhijit Muhurtham, Sayankala, Pradosha Kala) or type your exact custom start/end time and family astrologer notes.
What is your cancellation and reschedule policy? +
You receive a 100% full refund if cancelled more than 48 hours prior to the puja. Cancellations between 24–48 hours incur a nominal 10% fee. You can also reschedule your booking once at zero penalty if requested at least 24 hours in advance.
Will the Purohit bring all required Puja Samagri? +
You have full flexibility! During Step 2, you can choose "Include Complete Puja Samagri Kit" so that our certified Purohit brings pure, shastra-compliant items. Alternatively, you can arrange items yourself using the checklist provided on the puja details page.
How are Purohits verified on PurohitMitra? +
Every scholar on PurohitMitra is thoroughly vetted by our Vedic Advisory Board. We verify their Veda Pathashala certifications, lineage (Gurukula tradition), ritual knowledge, background checks, and minimum 5+ years of verified practical experience.
What payment methods are supported? +
We support all major Indian payment methods powered by 256-bit SSL encryption: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, WhatsApp Pay), Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), and Net Banking across 50+ major banks.
Do I need an account to browse pujas and purohits? +
No! You can freely browse all pujas, verified purohit profiles, authentic mantras, and transparent pricing without logging in. You will only be prompted to enter your phone number and OTP when confirming a booking or viewing your private devotee portal.
